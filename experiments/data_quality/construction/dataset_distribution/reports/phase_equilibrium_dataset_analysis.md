# Phase-equilibrium dataset coverage

The statistics below are computed from the four registered workbooks and use unordered, canonical-SMILES system identities.

## VLE

The dataset contains 27,304 observations: 22,760 binary points from 693 systems and 4,544 ternary points from 107 systems. Ternary observations represent 16.6% of the VLE records.

The binary and ternary subsets contain 328 and 111 distinct canonical components, respectively. Temperature and pressure sampling are strongly nonuniform, so densely populated operating regions coexist with sparse state-space tails.

Among the 107 ternary systems, 26 (24.3%) have all three constituent binary subsystems represented, whereas 5 (4.7%) have none. Overall, 58.9% have at most one observed binary subsystem. This limited overlap makes multicomponent prediction more demanding than interpolation among completely observed binary relations.

## LLE

The dataset contains 19,844 observations: 5,983 binary points from 394 systems and 13,861 ternary points from 986 systems. Ternary observations represent 69.8% of the LLE records.

The binary and ternary subsets contain 252 and 345 distinct canonical components, respectively. Temperature and pressure sampling are strongly nonuniform, so densely populated operating regions coexist with sparse state-space tails.

Among the 986 ternary systems, 1 (0.1%) have all three constituent binary subsystems represented, whereas 455 (46.1%) have none. Overall, 89.5% have at most one observed binary subsystem. This limited overlap makes multicomponent prediction more demanding than interpolation among completely observed binary relations.
