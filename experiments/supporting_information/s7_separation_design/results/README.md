# Common-vapour-pressure sensitivity archive

`common-psat-20260928/` is the frozen output of the S7.3 sensitivity protocol. It keeps the
17-point VLE tables, pure-component pressure checks, fixed-state decomposition, per-reference
results, figures, and the protocol and result reports. The protocol fixes the extractive
column at 183 stages, reflux 48, feed stage 159, solvent stage 16, and compares the Li (2013)
and Huang (2015) sec-butyl-acetate correlations while holding the alcohol and DMF correlations
common.

The report records that the Li correlation is extrapolated above its stated range and that the
Huang validation range is not independently verified. Do not treat the archived sensitivity
curves as new experimental data or as a replacement for a DWSIM recalculation.
